Structural definition of the triad and the psychoacoustic differentiation of modes

4.1 Structural Definition of the Triad and the Psychoacoustic Differentiation of Modes

In classical and functional harmonic theory, a chord is built by stacking intervals of a third. The basic unit that forms harmony is the triad, which consists of an arrangement of three specific notes: root (R), third (3rd) and fifth (5th).
