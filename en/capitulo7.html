10.1 Anatomy of Classical Resolutions: Perfect, Imperfect and Plagal Cadences

If chords are words, cadences are the punctuation of the musical text. They tell us whether a phrase has ended, whether there is a pause to breathe, or whether we have been fooled by a false resolution.
